# Copy Voice: Write Like a Human

**Status:** convention for RevealUI copy. Applies to any agent writing
customer-facing text for RevealUI.

## The rule

Marketing and product copy reads the way a person talks, not the way a model
compresses. Prefer full sentences with real verbs: "every agent is a
governed and audited user that lives on your infrastructure", not "every
agent a governed, audited user, on infrastructure you own".

## Word-choice checks before shipping any copy

1. **Full clauses with real verbs.** No verbless appositive fragments ("X, a
   Y, on Z").
2. **"and" over comma-stacked adjectives.** "governed and audited user", not
   "governed, audited user".
3. **Possessive and concrete.** "your infrastructure", not "infrastructure
   you own".
4. **No jargon in identity sentences.** Terms like "provider-agnostic" move
   to support lines in plain words ("runs on any AI provider you choose").
5. **No repetition inside a line.** Avoid phrasing like "your data and your
   audit trail never leave your infrastructure": repetitive and close to
   meaningless. Prefer a concrete image over abstract sovereignty language.
   A receipt beats an audit trail.
6. **No em dashes** (see `no-em-dashes.md`); split into real sentences
   instead.
7. Read it aloud. If a human would not say it to a prospect, rewrite it.

## Canonical positioning copy

Use these exact forms on public surfaces:

- Sentence: "RevealUI is the self-hosted runtime where your business and the
  AI agents that run it live under one roof. Every agent is a governed and
  audited user that lives on your infrastructure."
- Foil: "If an agent did it, there's a receipt."
- Foil, expanded variant for subheads: "Every agent action leaves a receipt
  you can check."
- Support line: "It runs on any AI provider you choose."
